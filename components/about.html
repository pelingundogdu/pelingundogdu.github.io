Research Interests

  • Explainable Neural Network An artificial neural network architecture or framework designed to produce human-interpretable explanations alongside its predictions to reveal how and why it reached a specific decision.
  • Trajectory (from healthy cells to cancer) The step-by-step biological timeline that tracks how a healthy cell gradually mutates, adapts, and transforms into a fully cancerous cell.
  • Variational Neural Network A probabilistic neural network that maps input data into a parameterized distribution over a latent space, allowing it to quantify uncertainty and generate new, realistic data samples.
  • Single Cell RNA Sequencing A genomic technique that measures the gene expression profile of individual cells within a tissue sample that enable to uncover cellular diversity, identify rare cell types, and map complex cell state transitions.
